LegallyBound is an AI-powered contract risk analysis system built to make legal document review faster, smarter, and privacy-friendly. The platform can analyze commercial contracts, identify risky clauses such as liability, indemnification, non-compete, and IP ownership terms, and provide real-time insights through semantic search and risk classification. It combines TinyLlama, Legal-BERT, and a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline with DuckDB vector search to deliver accurate clause extraction and contextual legal understanding, all while running locally to keep sensitive contract data private.

One of the most interesting parts of this project is the custom fine-tuning approach used for TinyLlama. Instead of training the entire model, different transformer layer combinations were tested to understand which layers actually help the model learn legal language better. After multiple experiments, the best results came from fine-tuning only the odd-numbered lower layers 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9 while keeping the layers in between frozen. This “Alternating Odd” setup helped the model understand complex legal wording and contract structure more effectively without losing its general language capabilities. The idea was that the frozen layers act like stabilizers, preserving what the model already knows while the trained layers specialize in legal reasoning. This approach achieved a BERTScore F1 of 84.32%, outperforming traditional fine-tuning methods and improving the model’s ability to generate more accurate and legally meaningful summaries.
